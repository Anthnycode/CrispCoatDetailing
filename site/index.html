What is PPF?

Paint protection film is a thick, clear urethane film that gets cut to fit each panel and laid right over your factory paint. It's the same idea as a screen protector for your phone. Rocks, sand, bug guts, shopping carts and car-wash swirls hit the film, not the clear coat.

The top layer heals itself. Light scratches and swirl marks fade away with heat from the sun or warm water. Clear film is close to invisible on gloss paint, color film changes the look and protects at the same time, and both come off clean when you want them gone.

PPF vs. vinyl wrap: vinyl only changes the color. PPF is about three times thicker and self-healing, so even color PPF protects the paint underneath.

  • Stops rock chipsFreeway gravel and road debris dent the film instead of chipping your paint.
  • Self-healingFine scratches and wash swirls disappear with heat.
  • Clear or colorKeep your factory paint, or change it with gloss, satin, metallic or color-shift film.
  • Works with ceramicCoat over the film for easy rinsing and water beading.
  • Pick your coverageFront bumper, partial front, full front end, high-impact areas or the full car.

    Questions

    Do I need to give you water or power?

    No. The truck carries its own water tank, pressure washer and generator. Just make sure there's room to work around the car.

    How long does it take?

    A full detail usually runs 3 to 5 hours. Interior only is about 2. RVs take 4 to 6 hours, and big Class A coaches can take the whole day.

    Which size is my vehicle?

    Sedan is cars and coupes. Mid Size is small SUVs and crossovers. SUV / Truck / 3rd Row is pickups, full-size SUVs and anything with a third row. Not sure? Pick the closer one and we'll sort it out when we confirm.

    What's the difference between Plus and Premium?

    Plus adds iron decontamination, a clay bar with a 3 week sealant, trim dressing, a full interior blow out and a scent of your choice. Premium is the full reset: a 3 month sealant, spot buffing, engine bay, interior steam clean, stain removal, seat shampoo or leather conditioning, and every carpet shampooed.

    Should I get paint correction before ceramic?

    Yes. A coating locks in whatever is under it, swirls included, so correction comes first. Ceramic lasts 2 to 5 years depending on the product and how you wash it. It won't stop rock chips or deep scratches.

    How often should I get a detail?

    Daily drivers that see a lot of dust do best every 2 to 3 months. Garaged cars can go every quarter. Out here in the Inland Empire, decontaminating the paint every 3 to 4 months keeps it smooth.

    Do you do maintenance plans?

    Yes. Monthly maintenance is $80 a month, and your second month's detail is free when you sign up.
